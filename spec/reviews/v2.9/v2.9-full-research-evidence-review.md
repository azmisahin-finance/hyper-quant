# HYPER-QUANT v2.9 — Full Research Evidence Review

Status: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`

## Scope

This review records the implementation of the v2.9 full research-evidence reference boundary. It links the finalized research campaign, controlled holdout result, selected out-of-sample return series, walk-forward validation, CSCV/PBO diagnostics, PSR/DSR diagnostics, regime coverage, cost stress, and statistical decision into one tamper-evident evidence object.

## Implemented controls

- PSR is computed from the selected return series and benchmark rather than accepted as analyst-supplied evidence.
- DSR is recomputed from selected returns and committed trial count and must match the finalized campaign diagnostic.
- Campaign and holdout identities are cross-checked before evidence construction.
- Holdout evidence must be `PASS` and selection evidence must exactly match the finalized campaign.
- Walk-forward fold count and effective OOS opportunities are carried into the evidence package.
- PBO/CSCV is cross-checked against the finalized campaign diagnostic.
- Regime identifiers are canonicalized and bound to the evidence package.
- A hash over the canonical evidence object makes subsequent mutation detectable.
- A durable append-only evidence ledger prevents multiple final evidence packages for one campaign and serializes concurrent writes.

## Adversarial coverage

The implementation test suite verifies:

- campaign metric substitution rejection;
- evidence-package tampering rejection;
- concurrent duplicate evidence commits are serialized;
- PSR/DSR are recomputed from underlying return data;
- campaign and holdout linkage cannot be swapped silently.

## Residual proof obligations

The reference implementation does not claim physical isolation of final holdout storage, production process isolation, independent research infrastructure, live venue reconciliation, or live signing authority. Those remain outside the non-live v2.9 reference boundary.
