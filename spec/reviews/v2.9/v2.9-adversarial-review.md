## Research validation vertical slice — adversarial update

The implementation now contains deterministic reference controls for:

- non-overlapping walk-forward folds with explicit purge and embargo separation;
- CSCV/PBO computation from candidate return paths, with a deterministic combination cap and negative-logit overfit classification;
- declared regime-segment coverage computed only over explicitly evaluated indices;
- research evidence binding that requires walk-forward, PBO/CSCV, and regime-coverage fields rather than an unqualified scalar PBO input.

Adversarial checks include overlapping walk-forward windows, combination-cap exhaustion, deterministic PBO replay, and regime-boundary violations. These are implementation proofs, not empirical evidence of strategy profitability.
